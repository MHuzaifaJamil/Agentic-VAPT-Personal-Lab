# Risk Register — Autonomous Agentic VAPT System

Likelihood/Impact: **L/M/H**. Severity is a qualitative combination, not a formula.
"Status" reflects what this planning phase has actually done about each risk — a
requirement reference means it's mitigated *on paper*; "Residual/Accepted" means the
risk was explicitly weighed and kept as a known trade-off, not overlooked.

## Risks Explicitly Not Mitigated By Design (documented trade-offs)

Three risks above — **RISK-UNCENSOREDGATE**, **RISK-TIER2RESIDUAL**, and
**RISK-UNBOUNDEDAUTONOMY** — remain open by deliberate operator decision rather than
oversight. They are the highest-severity items in this register and the ones most
worth revisiting if the intended use case ever shifts from a personal/lab environment
toward engagements involving other parties' infrastructure or contractual liability.
