# Risk Register — Autonomous Agentic VAPT System

Likelihood/Impact: **L/M/H**. Severity is a qualitative combination, not a formula.
"Status" reflects what this planning phase has actually done about each risk — a
requirement reference means it's mitigated *on paper*; "Residual/Accepted" means the
risk was explicitly weighed and kept as a known trade-off, not overlooked.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Mitigation | Status |

| RISK-SWAPWEAR | Daily hibernation cycles (paging 3.5-4.5 GB to NVMe swap) accelerate SSD wear over the device's operational life. | L-M (depends on usage frequency) | M | Medium | NFR-REL-05 (log cumulative swap write volume for visibility) — no hard limit imposed | Partially mitigated (visibility only) |
| RISK-DBLOCKCONTENTION | SQLite's default journal mode blocks concurrent reads (e.g., a `status` query) during a write. | M | L | Low | DR-CONCURRENCY-01 (WAL mode) | Mitigated |

| RISK-TOOLDECAY | `nuclei` templates, CVE feeds, and wordlists go stale over time with no update mechanism defined. | H (over time) | M | Medium | None — explicitly deferred (OPS-MAINT-01) | **Deferred, not mitigated** — out of scope for this planning phase |

Files created here don't automatically exist there. | H (certain, until transferred) | M | Medium | None yet — flagged as a dependency in `08-Assumptions-Constraints-Dependencies.md` | **Open — operator action required** (transfer this doc set, and ideally the whole `claude-bug-bounty` toolkit, to the target machine) |

| RISK-MADVISEPERM | `process_madvise(MADV_PAGEOUT)` requires `CAP_SYS_PTRACE`/`CAP_SYS_NICE`, which the least-privileged agent process (NFR-SEC-03) does not hold by design — the call would fail with `EPERM` if not addressed. | H (certain, if unaddressed) | H | High | FR-ENV-13 (narrow `setcap`/`sudoers`-scoped helper), cgroup v2 fallback if unavailable | Mitigated (finding C-15) |
| RISK-STALESOCKETS | Freezing desktop apps for 10-12 hours lapses their network/IPC sessions; on resume, apps may show reconnect/re-auth prompts or, in rare cases, discard state if the app itself forces a reload on stale-session detection. | H (network lapse is near-certain over that duration) | L-M | Medium | FR-ENV-14 (SLA reframed to process-memory guarantee only, reconnect logged as expected/informational) | Mitigated at the SLA/documentation level (finding C-16) — the underlying network lapse itself is not preventable |
| RISK-NOISYYIELD | A naive "yield" definition (non-empty output / exit 0) lets noisy tools (`ffuf`, `gobuster`) reset the zero-yield circuit breaker indefinitely, burning the entire 30-task-per-target budget on one unproductive target. | H (if unaddressed, common with wildcard/soft-404 responses) | M | Medium-High | FR-COUNCIL-11a + `discovered_entities` state-delta ledger (DR-SCHEMA-12) | Mitigated (finding C-17) |
| RISK-MEMSETTLERACE | Spawning the next model process immediately after the outbound one exits risks a transient window where the kernel hasn't finished reclaiming pages, potentially tripping the already-thin 1.5 GB safety margin. | M | M | Medium | FR-GATE-10/IR-ENGINE-06 (`MemAvailable` poll gate before spawn, 5s bound, degrades gracefully) | Mitigated (finding C-18) |
| RISK-ORPHANPROC | A tool that spawns worker sub-processes (`nmap`, `hydra`, etc.) leaves orphaned children running past `abort` if the kill-switch targets only the recorded parent PID — silently violating the 20-second SLA. | H (common for multi-process tools) | H | High | FR-TOOL-04a (spawn in a new session), SEC-KILL-01/02 (kill the whole process group) | Mitigated (finding C-19) |
| RISK-DBLOCKED | Concurrent CLI invocations (`pause`/`abort` writing state while the orchestrator is mid-commit) can raise `database is locked` under WAL mode alone, failing the exact command meant to regain control. | M | H | Medium-High | DR-CONCURRENCY-03 (`PRAGMA busy_timeout = 5000`) | Mitigated (finding C-20) |
| RISK-REDACTMISMATCH | Regex/pattern-based redaction addressing can match the wrong occurrence of a repeated token, or fail under irregular line breaks, risking the wrong (or no) value restored into a client-facing report. | M | H | High | `redaction_map` revised to exact byte-offset + content-hash addressing, with a hard failure on hash mismatch | Mitigated (finding C-21) |
| RISK-STRUCTOUTPUT | Quantized 7B-8B models under plain prompting can emit malformed or non-schema-conforming JSON for tool calls, CVSS metrics, or gate decisions, breaking essentially every LLM-to-code handoff. | H (common on smaller/quantized models) | H | High | `response_format={"type":"json_object"}` + mandatory Python schema validator + bounded 2-retry repair loop (IR-STRUCTURED) | Mitigated (finding C-22) |
| RISK-STALEREDACT | Post-processing an LLM's free-form draft for secrets it already saw unredacted is fragile — paraphrasing/reformatting can defeat an exact-string scan, leaking a secret into a pre-approval draft in a form no filter catches. | M | H | High | Redaction moved to before the Reporter LLM call — it never sees the real value, so its output can't leak a reformatted version of it | Mitigated (finding C-23) |
| RISK-OPERATORWASTE | The Operator can't see current opt-in flag state, so it may repeatedly propose a disabled Tier 2 category, burning task-budget slots on predictable refusals. | M | L | Low-Medium | Flag state now included in the Operator's per-task context (FR-COUNCIL-07 revised) | Mitigated (finding C-24) |
| RISK-REPORTSCHEMA | The original `reports` schema (keyed only by `engagement_id`) couldn't represent `12-Report-Formatting-Rules.md`'s own established format — one report per confirmed finding plus one consolidated informational register — a real modeling gap that would have surfaced the first time a multi-finding engagement tried to generate reports. | H (certain, on first multi-finding engagement) | H | High | `reports.finding_id` + `reports.document_type` added, with a partial unique index enforcing one `INFO_REGISTER` row per engagement (DR-SCHEMA-11 revised) | Mitigated (finding C-25) |
| RISK-UNGROUNDEDREPORT | The Reporter LLM can confidently cite a URL/endpoint/detail that sounds plausible but never appeared in the evidence it was shown — nothing previously verified report content against its own source evidence. | M | H | High | Deterministic grounding check (IR-GROUND-01..03) before a report can leave `DRAFT_PENDING_APPROVAL`; bounded retry then `BLOCKED_UNGROUNDED` on persistent failure | Mitigated (finding C-26) |
| RISK-DEADTARGETWASTE | Without a failure-specific circuit breaker, an unreachable target (network down, firewall block, taken offline mid-engagement) would only incidentally trip the zero-yield breaker, wasting task-budget slots and leaving the audit trail unable to distinguish "unproductive" from "unreachable." | M | M | Medium | Separate failure-based circuit breaker, 3 consecutive network errors/timeouts, marks the target `UNREACHABLE` distinctly (FR-COUNCIL-11b) | Mitigated (finding C-27) |
| RISK-NORATELIMIT | Nothing previously limited how fast the Operator could spawn tool invocations against a target — a real operational/legal risk given RoEs commonly require rate limits, and a real DoS-adjacent risk for an unattended 12-hour autonomous run. | M | M-H | Medium-High | Per-target, per-category rate limiting (10/s default, 1/s high-risk categories) at the bridge level (FR-TOOL-14, IR-BRIDGE-05) | Mitigated (finding C-28) |
| RISK-CONTEXTGROWTH | The Operator stays resident for an entire per-target loop (up to 30 tasks) with no stated context-window management strategy against its 16k ceiling (`FR-GATE-07`) — comparable systems claim a compression technique but it could not be verified as a concrete, adoptable mechanism. | M (grows with task count) | M | Medium | None — genuinely unresolved | **Open** (finding C-29 / Open Item H, `10-Decision-Log-and-Open-Questions.md`) |

## Risks Explicitly Not Mitigated By Design (documented trade-offs)

Three risks above — **RISK-UNCENSOREDGATE**, **RISK-TIER2RESIDUAL**, and
**RISK-UNBOUNDEDAUTONOMY** — remain open by deliberate operator decision rather than
oversight. They are the highest-severity items in this register and the ones most
worth revisiting if the intended use case ever shifts from a personal/lab environment
toward engagements involving other parties' infrastructure or contractual liability.
